Apple Intelligence

Question generation uses the on-device model when it is available on this Mac. No model download or endpoint configuration is required in this app.

Controls

Keyboard: WASD or arrow keys. Escape pauses. Xbox: D-pad / left stick to navigate, A to select, B to go back, LB / RB to switch screens, and right stick to scroll. Left / right adjusts a focused setting. During play, the left stick drives and Menu pauses.
