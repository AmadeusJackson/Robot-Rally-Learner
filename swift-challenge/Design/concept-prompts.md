# Exploratory concepts (not production assets)

Both drafts used the built-in ImageGen tool with transparent-background output. The final icon uses original SVG geometry from `build-icon.py`, not either generated raster. These prompts record the exploratory use of AI.

## Initial generation

Use case: logo-brand. Create a production foreground artwork layer for Robot Rally, a native Mac robotics learning game. One distinctive, highly simplified competition rover, seen perfectly top-down, centered and rotated 25 degrees clockwise so its front points upper-right. Not a robot face, not a toy car. Design it as carefully drawn flat industrial graphic illustration with a few large filled shapes, crisp antialiased edges and meticulous geometric symmetry. Four chunky charcoal mecanum wheels sit at the four corners, each with exactly two broad diagonal ivory roller marks. The short rectangular chassis is warm ivory; vivid burnt-orange rounded bumper bars at front and rear wrap slightly around its corners, recognizably a small competition robot. A single blue inset rectangular controller module sits in the center. No other detail. A strong confident silhouette that stays legible at 32px. Palette: burnt orange #F47C35, warm ivory #F3EEE3, charcoal #202D39, muted blue #4282B0. Actual transparent background, square 1024x1024 canvas, artwork occupies about 72% of both canvas dimensions and has generous clear margin. No enclosing icon tile: Apple Icon Composer will provide the background and final mask. No text, no letters, no numbers, no smile, no eyes, no antenna, no gears, no book, no lightbulb, no checkmark, no sparkle, no circuit traces, no microscopic mechanical clutter. No cast shadows, no glow, no bevels, no gradients, no photographic textures, no highlights, no dramatic 3D lighting. Keep this a deliberate, clean flat illustration suitable for layering in an app icon, not a generic glossy AI mascot.

## Concept revision

Refine this app-icon foreground, preserving its palette, four mecanum wheels, orange front/rear bumpers, top-down view, diagonal upper-right heading, transparent background, and generous margins. Specific corrections: make the rover SHORTER AND WIDER, an almost square competition-robot footprint, not a car; shorten the distance between front and rear wheels by about 30 percent. Move all four wheels to four symmetric corners with identical widths and lengths; each wheel has two clean thick diagonal roller marks, with the left and right roller slants mirrored as in mecanum wheels. The chassis is one large warm-ivory rounded square. Replace the long blue windshield-like panel with a much smaller blue SQUARE electronics module at center (about one third of chassis width), with a single broad ivory slot cut through its center; this must read as a robot control module, not a car windshield. Keep the single-object silhouette approximately 72 percent of the square canvas. Make every filled shape uniform flat color with absolutely no gradients, grain, texture, glow, drop shadows, or bevels. Crisp geometrically intentional shapes, a beautifully balanced production icon illustration. No text, no face, no eyes, no antennas, no extra objects. The result should feel like a carefully designed competition rover symbol rather than a stock race-car icon.
